## What Okendo does

**Okendo** sells five products that can be used on their own or together: Reviews, Loyalty, Referrals, Quizzes and Surveys. According to the [Okendo pricing page](https://www.okendo.io/pricing/):

- **Reviews**: product reviews, site reviews, review capture form.
- **Loyalty**: earn and redeem rules, custom loyalty page, VIP tiers.
- **Referrals**: referral email flows, fraud protection, automated workflows.
- **Quizzes**: personalized product recommendations, AI-assisted quiz creation.
- **Surveys**: question logic, shareable link surveys, post-review and post-checkout surveys.

Okendo also offers bundles (Attract, Convert, Retain) and a Platform Scale offer with a fixed annual price and no order cap. The page says bundles cost 30% less than buying the products separately.

## Okendo pricing (Reviews product)

| Essential | $19/month | $204/year | Up to 200 | Everything in Free |
| Growth | $119/month | $1,308/year | Up to 1,500 | AI review summaries and keywords, TikTok Shop integration, Q&A widget |
| Power | $299/month | $3,288/year | Up to 3,500 | Review campaigns, advanced CSS editor, email and SMS integrations, advanced reporting, managed onboarding |

Pricing checked on the [Okendo Shopify App Store listing](https://apps.shopify.com/okendo-reviews) on Oct 18, 2026. The [Okendo pricing page](https://www.okendo.io/pricing/) shows no prices: loyalty, bundles and the full platform go through the sales team.

Key billing points, again from the pricing page:

- Price depends on the number of orders per month. If you go over your quota, you upgrade or buy a one-time top-up of order credits.
- Platform and Bundle subscriptions normally require annual terms. Individual products can be monthly or annual.
- Quizzes and Referrals are monthly, cancellable anytime, with no implementation fee.

## Integrations

The [Shopify listing](https://apps.shopify.com/okendo-reviews) lists: Checkout, customer accounts, Shopify POS, Shopify Flow, Google, Meta, TikTok, Klaviyo, Gorgias and Postscript. Email and SMS integrations are part of the Power plan of the Reviews product.

## Who it's for

- **Shopify DTC brands** that want reviews, loyalty and referrals from a single vendor.
- **Stores just starting out**: the Free plan covers up to 50 orders a month.
- **High-volume brands**: above 3,500 orders a month, you need the quote-based Platform Scale offer.

## Strengths and limitations

**Strengths:** five complementary products, a free plan, a 14-day trial, Klaviyo, Gorgias and Postscript integrations.

**Limitations:** the price climbs quickly with volume ($19 to $299/month between 200 and 3,500 orders), email and SMS integrations are limited to the Power plan, the app is in English and products other than reviews have no public pricing.
