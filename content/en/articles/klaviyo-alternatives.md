## Why look for a Klaviyo alternative

**Klaviyo** bills email by active contacts: according to its ["How Klaviyo pricing works"](https://www.klaviyo.com/platform/how-klaviyo-pricing-works) page, only reachable contacts count, and unsubscribed or deleted contacts are not billed. A growing list drives your bill up, even if you don't send more emails. SMS is billed separately.

On its [Shopify App Store listing](https://apps.shopify.com/klaviyo-email-marketing), email is free up to 250 contacts, then the Email plan starts at $20/month for 251 to 500 contacts.

## What to know at 10,000 contacts

None of these tools shows a readable price at 10,000 contacts outside its calculator as of Oct 18, 2026. Here are the verified rules that matter at this volume:

- **Brevo**: the [pricing grid](https://brevo.com/pricing.md) ties a maximum contact count to each email tier (500 contacts at 5,000 emails/month, 1,500 at 10,000, 2,500 at 15,000). For 10,000 contacts you need at least the 20,000 emails/month tier, whose price is not shown in the grid.
- **Omnisend**: the price follows a list-size slider; the Standard plan includes 6,000 emails/month at the first tier (500 contacts), and the Pro plan (from $59/month for 2,500 contacts) is unlimited with fair use, according to its [pricing page](https://www.omnisend.com/pricing/).
- **Mailchimp**: the [pricing page](https://mailchimp.com/pricing/marketing/) offers 15% off the first 12 months from 10,000 contacts.
- **Klaviyo**: the price at this tier only appears in the calculator on its [pricing page](https://www.klaviyo.com/pricing).

Enter your real list size and send volume in each calculator before comparing.

## The alternatives in detail

### Brevo

Brevo bills by emails sent, not by contacts. According to its [pricing grid](https://brevo.com/pricing.md), Starter starts at €7/month, Standard at €15/month and Professional at €350/month billed monthly, with 10% off annually. Automated flows (welcome, abandoned cart) require the Standard plan. On Shopify, Brevo runs through the [Brevo PushOwl](https://apps.shopify.com/pushowl) app. Details in our [Klaviyo vs Brevo](/en/email-marketing/klaviyo-vs-brevo/) comparison.

### Omnisend

The free plan includes email, web push and SMS (paid per message). Standard costs $16/month for 500 contacts, Pro $59/month for 2,500 contacts, according to the [pricing page](https://www.omnisend.com/pricing/). Abandoned cart, welcome and post-purchase flows are included. Full details in our [Omnisend review](/en/email-marketing/omnisend-review-pricing/).

### Splio

Splio presents itself as a CRM platform for retail: marketing automation, Customer Data Platform, loyalty and mobile wallets, with point-of-sale integrations. No public pricing; everything goes through a demo. The [Splio website](https://splio.com) doesn't mention a dedicated Shopify connector: confirm with their team.

### Mailchimp

The free plan covers 250 contacts and 500 emails per month. Essentials starts at $13/month, Standard at $20/month and Premium at $350/month (prices shown for 12 months), according to the [pricing page](https://mailchimp.com/pricing/marketing/). Check the available Shopify integration before committing.

## How to choose

- **Large list, few sends**: **Brevo**, whose price follows email volume rather than list size.
- **Stay close to Klaviyo on Shopify, with SMS and web push**: **Omnisend**.
- **Simple newsletter**: **Mailchimp**.
- **Physical stores and a loyalty program**: **Splio**, on demo.
