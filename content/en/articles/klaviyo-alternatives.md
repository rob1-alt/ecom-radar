## Why leave Klaviyo

Klaviyo charges by contact count. A fast-growing list drives your bill up, even if you don't send more emails.

## The alternatives

| Alternative | Best for | Price at 10,000 contacts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Brevo** | French stores | ~€65/month |
| **Omnisend** | Email + SMS | ~$115/month |
| **Splio** | Omnichannel retail | Custom quote |
| **Mailchimp** | Simple newsletters | ~$100/month |
