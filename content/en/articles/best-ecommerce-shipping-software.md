## The details

### Sendcloud

**Sendcloud** connects your store to dozens of carriers, prints labels in bulk, sends tracking updates to your customers and runs a returns portal.

### Boxtal

**Boxtal** compares carrier rates and gives you negotiated prices with no minimum volume. Ideal when you're starting out.

### Shippingbo

**Shippingbo** centralizes orders from your store and your marketplaces, then handles picking, packing and shipping.

### Cubyn

**Cubyn** stores your products, then picks, packs and ships your orders. You no longer need to run a warehouse.
