## The tools in detail

### Klaviyo

Its [official Shopify app](https://apps.shopify.com/klaviyo-email-marketing) syncs the store's tags, catalog and coupons. It offers email and SMS campaigns and flows, multichannel segmentation with AI-generated segments, product recommendations, send time optimization and A/B testing. Predictive analytics is listed in the Email plan.

On pricing: email is free up to 250 contacts and 500 sends a month. Once you hit the limit, [sending is paused](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/56142459211675) until the next month or until you upgrade. The free plan also includes 150 SMS credits. The Email plan then starts at $20/month (251 to 500 contacts), the SMS plan at $15/month for 1,250 credits.

### Omnisend

Its free plan includes email (500/month), web push (500/month) and SMS (paid per message). The [pricing page](https://www.omnisend.com/pricing/) shows:

- **Standard**: $16/month for 500 contacts and 6,000 emails a month.
- **Pro**: $59/month for 2,500 contacts, unlimited emails (fair use).
- **Custom**: quote-based above 150,000 active profiles.

SMS starts at $0.007 per message in the US. Abandoned cart, welcome and post-purchase flows are included, and Omnisend advertises 24/7 support plus an MCP integration to build campaigns from ChatGPT or Claude.

### Brevo

Brevo has a "free forever" plan limited to 300 emails a day. Its paid plans are framed by email volume: Starter starts at 5,000 emails/month (email and SMS, SMS credits sold separately) and Pro at 150,000 emails/month, with WhatsApp and advanced e-commerce features. Euro amounts were not displayed on the [pricing page](https://www.brevo.com/fr/pricing/) when we checked: simulate your volume directly on the page. Brevo connects to Shopify among more than 150 tools.

### Splio

Splio presents itself as an "AI-first" CRM platform: marketing automation, Customer Data Platform, loyalty, predictive AI and mobile wallets. It targets retail and offers point-of-sale (PoS) integrations to measure ROI online and offline. No public pricing: everything goes through a demo. The [Splio website](https://splio.com) doesn't mention a dedicated Shopify connector, so confirm with their team.

### Mailchimp

The free plan covers 250 contacts and 500 emails a month (250 a day max), with a single user. Essentials starts at $13/month and Standard at $20/month (12-month offers), Premium at $350/month, according to the [pricing page](https://mailchimp.com/pricing/marketing/). It's an option for a simple newsletter; check which Shopify integration is available before committing.

## How to choose

### You're starting with a small list

Compare the free plans. **Klaviyo**, **Omnisend** and **Mailchimp** all stop at 250 contacts and 500 emails a month. **Brevo** allows up to 300 emails a day, which leaves more room to send often.

### You want email and SMS in one place

**Omnisend** and **Klaviyo** handle both in the same Shopify app. With **Brevo**, SMS is available from Starter with credits bought separately.

### You want to make the most of your Shopify data

**Klaviyo** syncs catalog, tags and coupons, and adds AI segments and predictive analytics. It costs more than **Omnisend** at the same list size ($20 vs $16 at the first tier).

### You have physical stores

**Splio** is built for retail with a loyalty module and PoS integrations. Plan a demo to get a price.
