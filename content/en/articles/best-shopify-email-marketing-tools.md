Prices are indicative; check the vendors' pricing pages.

## How to choose

### Your store makes less than €30,000 a month

**Brevo** or **Omnisend**. Basic flows (welcome, abandoned cart, post-purchase) are enough, and the bill stays low.

### Email drives more than 25% of your revenue

**Klaviyo**. Segmentation on Shopify data (margin, purchase frequency, repurchase prediction) pays for itself quickly.

### You have physical stores

**Splio** connects in-store and online purchases, with a built-in loyalty program.
