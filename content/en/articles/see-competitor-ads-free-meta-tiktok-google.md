## The 3 libraries at a glance

| Library | Platforms covered | Price | Account required | What it shows on top in Europe |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Meta Ad Library** | Facebook, Instagram and other Meta apps | Free | Not stated by Meta | All ads targeting the EU, run dates, targeting criteria (age, gender, location), people reached, 1-year archive |
| **TikTok Ad Library** | TikTok (ads + creator content disclosed as commercial) | Free | No | Payer, first and last shown dates, range of unique users, targeting, users by country, kept 1 year after last view |
| **Google Ads Transparency Center** | Search, Display, Gmail, YouTube | Free | Not stated by Google | Targeting information, total number of recipients per ad, ad topic |

Information checked on November 27, 2026 on [Meta's DSA announcement](https://about.fb.com/news/2023/08/new-features-and-additional-transparency-measures-as-the-digital-services-act-comes-into-effect/), [TikTok's help page on the Commercial Content Library](https://support.tiktok.com/en/account-and-privacy/personalized-ads-and-data/commercial-content-library) and [Google's ads transparency help page](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/13733850).

## Why does Europe see more than the US?

The Digital Services Act (DSA) requires large platforms to keep a public repository of their ads. For a European online store, that's an advantage: you see ads targeting Europe with more detail than elsewhere.

Meta announced it in August 2023: the library now displays and archives all ads that target people in the EU, with run dates, targeting criteria (age, gender, location) and information about who was reached. Before, this detail was limited to political and social-issue ads.

## How do you see a competitor's Facebook and Instagram ads?

### Meta Ad Library

Open [Meta's Ad Library](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/), pick the country and the "All ads" category, then type the competitor's page name. You see its creatives, copy and launch dates.

For an ad targeting the EU, Meta shows run dates, targeting criteria and information about the people reached. These ads are stored for a year, according to [Meta's announcement](https://about.fb.com/news/2023/08/new-features-and-additional-transparency-measures-as-the-digital-services-act-comes-into-effect/).

## How do you see a competitor's TikTok ads?

### TikTok Ad Library

The [Commercial Content Library](https://library.tiktok.com) covers any ad targeting the European Economic Area, Switzerland or the UK, published since October 1, 2022. No TikTok account is needed, says [TikTok's help center](https://support.tiktok.com/en/account-and-privacy/personalized-ads-and-data/commercial-content-library).

It's the most detailed of the three for a store owner:

- the advertiser and, if different, who paid (an agency for example);
- the first and last shown dates;
- an estimated range of unique users who saw the ad;
- the targeting (age, gender, location, interests);
- the countries where it ran, with the number of unique users per country.

You can filter by target country, ad type and date, and sort by audience size. An ad can take up to 24 hours to appear. The library also holds creator content disclosed as commercial (paid partnerships), with the tagged brand and views: handy to spot a competitor's influencers.

## How do you see a competitor's Google and YouTube ads?

### Google Ads Transparency Center

The [Google Ads Transparency Center](https://adstransparency.google.com) gathers ads served on Search, Display, Gmail and YouTube. You search by advertiser name or website name, then filter by date and targeted location, according to [Google's help page](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/13733850).

Google shows the legal name and location of verified advertisers ("verified" badge), or the payments profile name if they're not verified. For ads served in the European Economic Area, it adds targeting information, the total number of recipients of each ad and the ad's topic.

## What should you look for in a competitor's ads?

| Question | Where to look |
| --- | --- |
| Which products are they pushing? | Active creatives in all 3 libraries |
| Which ads do they keep running? | First shown date (Meta, TikTok): an ad running for weeks is being funded continuously, which doesn't prove it's profitable |
| Which audience do they target? | Targeting criteria (age, gender, location) on ads targeting Europe |
| How many people do they reach? | Range of unique users (TikTok), number of recipients (Google) |
| Which creators do they work with? | Creator commercial content in the TikTok library |

None of the three official pages checked lists the spend on a commercial ad. The number of people reached is a hint, not an amount.

## When should you switch to a paid tool?

Free libraries show the ads of an advertiser you already know, one platform at a time. ## How to choose

- You monitor 2 or 3 known competitors: the three free libraries are enough.
- You sell mostly through TikTok or influencers: start with the **TikTok Ad Library**, the most detailed.
- You run Google Shopping or Search ads: the **Google Ads Transparency Center** is the only one of the three covering Google.
- You're looking for products or brands you don't know yet: a paid tool like **TrendTrack**.
