Pricing checked on the [Loop pricing page](https://loopreturns.com/pricing), the [ReturnGO pricing page](https://returngo.ai/pricing/) and the Shopify App Store listings for [Loop](https://apps.shopify.com/loop-returns) and [ReturnGO](https://apps.shopify.com/returngo) on Nov 23, 2026. All prices are in US dollars, and both Shopify listings state billing in US dollars every 30 days.

## Loop's plans

As of Nov 23, 2026, the [Loop pricing page](https://loopreturns.com/pricing) shows only two options:

- **Checkout+**, presented as free ("free software + return shipping"): the returns software, domestic return labels for the US and Canada, Return Bars and advanced features. The page states that "not every merchant qualifies", with eligibility based on order and return volume; the [Shopify listing](https://apps.shopify.com/loop-returns) says it is "free for brands with 5k+ annual orders".
- **Returns**, from $340/month: up to 1,000 returns a year, Shop Now, instant exchange, bonus credit and fraud prevention.

That is a change from our Oct 18, 2026 reading, when the same page showed three options: Checkout+, **Essential from $155/month** and **Advanced from $340/month**. Essential is gone, and the remaining paid offer now publishes an included volume (1,000 returns a year) that the page did not mention before.

In practice, Loop's paid entry point went from $155 to $340/month, and 1,000 returns a year works out to about **83 returns a month**. The price beyond that volume is not published: the figure is still a "starting at" price with a demo to book, and the page mentions no per-return fee.

## ReturnGO's plans

[ReturnGO](https://returngo.ai/pricing/) shows three options:

- **Premium**, $147/month: embedded self-service return portal, product and variant exchanges, refunds, gift cards and store credit, approval automations, shipping integrations and Shop Now exchanges, 2 users. Instant exchange is marked as an add-on (paid option) on this plan in the comparison table.
- **Pro**, $297/month: advanced automation, item validation, packing slips, unlimited languages, ERP integrations, API and webhooks, 3 users.
- **Enterprise**, custom pricing: order tracking portal, multi-store plans, omnichannel support and a dedicated account manager. The annual volume stated for this plan differs between the pricing card and the comparison table on the page: ask for it in writing before signing.

According to the [Shopify listing](https://apps.shopify.com/returngo), Premium includes 110 returns per month and Pro 300, with a 14-day free trial. The pricing page FAQ says ReturnGO is built to integrate with any ecommerce platform, with the free trial limited to Shopify self-service plans.

## The math at 200 returns a month

Using ReturnGO's pricing, here is what 200 returns a month cost:

- **Premium**: $147 + 90 extra returns × $1.25 = **$259.50/month**.
- **Pro**: $297/month, since 200 returns fit within the 300 included.

Above roughly 230 returns a month, Pro becomes cheaper than Premium ($147 + 120 × $1.25 = $297).

At Loop, the same volume means 2,400 returns a year, more than double the 1,000 returns included in the $340/month Returns offer. The cost of returns beyond the included volume is not published: at 200 returns a month, $340/month is the floor, with no published overage rate. Ask for a quote at your volume before comparing.

The other way round, a store with 50 returns a month fits inside Loop's 1,000 annual returns but still pays $340/month, against $147/month on ReturnGO Premium.

## When to choose Loop

- You mostly ship to the US or Canada and you qualify for Checkout+ (free, from 5,000 annual orders according to the Shopify listing).
- You want instant exchange, bonus credit and Shop Now in one plan, and the Returns budget (from $340/month) works for you.
- Your return portal can stay in English.

## When to choose ReturnGO

- You sell in France or elsewhere in Europe and want a portal in your customers' language (2 languages on Premium, unlimited on Pro).
- You want readable pricing: included returns and per-extra-return cost are published, which Loop does not publish.
- Your volume is under 300 returns a month: the paid entry point is $147/month against $340/month at Loop.
- You want to try before committing, with the 14-day free trial on Shopify.
