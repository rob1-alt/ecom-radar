## Head to head

| Criterion | **Alma** | **Klarna** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customer options | 2, 3 or 4 installments, or pay later (D+15, D+30) | Installments, pay in full, pay later or financing, chosen automatically by Klarna |
| Merchant payout | Full amount immediately | In your next Shopify Payments payout |
| Shopify integration | Free Alma ‑ Badge app (widget) and checkout extension | Through Shopify Payments; Klarna Payments module limited to merchants with a direct agreement |

Information checked on Nov 8, 2026 on [Alma's Shopify App Store listing](https://apps.shopify.com/alma-badge?locale=fr), [Alma's pricing page](https://almapay.com/pricing), [Klarna's Shopify Payments documentation](https://docs.klarna.com/platform-solutions/e-commerce-platforms/shopify/payments/shopify-payments) and [Klarna's Payments module documentation](https://docs.klarna.com/platform/shopify/payments/klarna-payments-module/).

## Alma

According to its [Shopify listing](https://apps.shopify.com/alma-badge?locale=fr), **Alma** lets your customers pay in 2, 3 or 4 installments or later (D+15, D+30), and you receive the full amount immediately. The Alma ‑ Badge app is free: it shows the payment options on product pages and in the cart. It is available in English only.

Worth knowing: in a reply to a review on the listing, Alma explains that its banking partners restrict which business types it can accept and that its credit risk procedures are strict. Not every store is eligible.

[Alma's pricing page](https://almapay.com/pricing) publishes the merchant fees, excluding VAT: 3.60% for pay in 2, 3 or 4 installments and 4.20% for pay later (D+15, D+30), of which 0.71% is the base fee; the rest can be shared with the customer, within regulatory limits. There is no subscription or setup fee, the standard offer covers up to €1M of turnover, and rates may be adjusted according to risk (automatic increase for American Express and business cards). On a €60 order that is €2.16 excl. VAT (3.60%) or €2.52 excl. VAT (4.20%) if you share nothing with the customer. The Badge app listing also shows 19 reviews with an average rating of 2.2 out of 5.

## Klarna

According to [Klarna's documentation](https://docs.klarna.com/platform-solutions/e-commerce-platforms/shopify/payments/shopify-payments), **Klarna** is added on Shopify as a local payment method of Shopify Payments. Your customers can pay in installments, in full, later or with financing, and funds arrive in your next Shopify Payments payout. You don't choose which options are shown: Klarna decides automatically.

The blocker for a French store: the list of eligible store countries (Austria, Germany, Italy, Netherlands, Spain, Sweden, United Kingdom, etc.) does not include France as of Nov 8, 2026. The [Klarna Payments module](https://docs.klarna.com/platform/shopify/payments/klarna-payments-module/) is limited to merchants with a direct Klarna agreement, and the documentation sends new integrations to Shopify Payments. Check your store's eligibility with Klarna before committing. Fees are not stated in this documentation.

## How to choose

- **Your Shopify store is based in France**: **Alma** is the most direct route, provided your business type is accepted.
- **Your store is based in a country Klarna lists** (Germany, Spain, Italy, etc.): **Klarna** through Shopify Payments avoids a separate contract.
- **Either way**, get the merchant fees in writing (Klarna's are not published in the documentation checked) and compare them with your average order value.

## The smart move

Turn on installment payments for your most expensive products and measure your conversion rate before and after, over comparable periods.
