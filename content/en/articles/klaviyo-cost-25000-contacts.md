| 35,000 | $550 | 350,000 |

Prices read in the calculator on [Klaviyo's pricing page](https://www.klaviyo.com/pricing) on Oct 18, 2026, Email plan, excluding tax and SMS.

At 25,000 active profiles, budget **$400/month, or $4,800 over 12 months**, before SMS. The 25,000-profile tier includes 250,000 sends, that is 10 emails per contact per month.

## How Klaviyo calculates your bill

According to [How Klaviyo pricing works](https://www.klaviyo.com/platform/how-klaviyo-pricing-works):

- **Active profiles.** Email is billed on reachable contacts. Unsubscribed and suppressed contacts don't count. Cleaning your list can therefore drop you a tier.
- **Send capacity.** Each tier includes a monthly send volume. With flexible sending on, if you exceed it, you can temporarily get the next tier's send capacity at your current unit rate. It returns to normal the next cycle.
- **Contact overages.** If your active profiles exceed your tier, the account moves to a compliant plan at the next billing cycle.
- **Auto downgrade.** If your active profiles fall below a tier, auto downgrade moves you to the lower tier at the next cycle. It can't run alongside send-based auto upgrades.
- **SMS billed separately.** SMS, MMS, WhatsApp and RCS are billed separately from email: you commit to a spend amount that burns down as you send, with rates that vary by message type and country.

These rules apply to month-to-month customers.

## What this means for a 25,000-contact list

1. **Count active profiles, not your raw list.** If part of your 25,000 contacts is unsubscribed or suppressed, you pay a lower tier.
2. **Add SMS separately.** The email price doesn't cover SMS. The SMS plan starts at $15/month for 1,250 credits on the [Shopify App Store listing](https://apps.shopify.com/klaviyo-email-marketing).
3. **Plan for growth.** If your list grows during the year, your plan moves up at the next cycle: calculate your 12-month bill with the tier you'll reach, not today's.

## Comparing with Omnisend and Brevo at the same volume

**Omnisend** sets its price with a list-size slider; above 150,000 contacts, it's a custom quote. The Standard plan allows up to 12 times your list size in emails per month. The Pro plan is unlimited (fair use up to 60 times your list size) and includes bonus SMS credits equal to your monthly bill, according to its [pricing page](https://www.omnisend.com/pricing/). Unsubscribed contacts aren't billed.

**Brevo** works differently: its paid plans are presented by email volume sent, not by contact count, according to its [pricing page](https://www.brevo.com/pricing/). At 25,000 contacts, your bill depends mostly on how often you send.
