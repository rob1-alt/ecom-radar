PayPlug is a French payment provider. Its pricing depends on an indicative annual turnover: Starter up to €100,000, Pro from €100,000 to €1,000,000, Enterprise above that with custom pricing.

## What the numbers show

### PayPlug Starter never beats Stripe on a consumer card

The rate is identical (1.5% + €0.25) and PayPlug adds €10 excl. VAT per month. Starter's appeal lies elsewhere: a French provider, with online and in-store payments on the same account (1.5% + €0.10 in store according to its page).

### PayPlug Pro pulls ahead above a certain volume

Against Stripe, the 0.4-point gap covers the €30 subscription from €7,500 in monthly sales. Against Mollie on Cartes Bancaires, the gap is only 0.1 point: you need more than €30,000 in monthly sales for PayPlug Pro to become cheaper.

### Mollie is the cheapest below €30,000 a month… if payments go through Cartes Bancaires

The 1.20% rate only applies to consumer Cartes Bancaires cards. A European consumer Visa or Mastercard costs 1.80% + €0.25, which makes Mollie the most expensive of the three in that case. Before choosing, check what share of your payments actually goes through the CB network.

### Foreign and business cards change the ranking

If you sell a lot outside Europe, PayPlug lists the lowest rate of the three (2.9% + €0.25, versus 3.15% at Stripe and 3.25% at Mollie). On business cards, PayPlug lists 2.5% + €0.25 and Mollie 2.90% + €0.25 for the EEA; we did not find an equivalent line on Stripe's page.

## What about disputes?

Stripe lists €20 per dispute received, plus €20 if you contest it. The PayPlug and Mollie pricing pages checked on November 26, 2026 show no dispute fee and no refund fee: ask for them in writing before signing. The breakdown by provider is in our article on [card chargeback costs](/en/payments/chargeback-fees-stripe-shopify-paypal-mollie/).

## How to choose

### You sell less than €5,000 a month

**Stripe** or **Mollie**: no subscription. Mollie is cheaper if most of your customers pay with Cartes Bancaires, Stripe if they mostly use foreign Visa or Mastercard cards.

### You sell between €7,500 and €30,000 a month

**PayPlug Pro** drops below Stripe, but **Mollie** stays cheaper on Cartes Bancaires payments. The choice depends on your CB / Visa-Mastercard split.

### You sell more than €30,000 a month

**PayPlug Pro** becomes the cheapest of the three on French consumer cards, based on the public rates. Above the volume pricing thresholds (over €100,000 at Mollie, over €1,000,000 in annual turnover at PayPlug), ask for a custom offer.
