## Step 1: the store (5 minutes)

With an extension like **Koala Inspector** or the **TrendTrack** one, note the theme, the installed apps (reviews, upsell, email marketing) and the featured products. The apps tell you a lot about the stack and the budget.

## Step 2: the ads (10 minutes)

Open the Meta Ad Library or the **TrendTrack** ad library. Note the ads that have been running for a long time: an ad that has been live for weeks is a profitable ad.

## Step 3: the emails (5 minutes + a few days)

Sign up for the newsletter and add a product to your cart without buying. You'll get the welcome flow and the abandoned cart flow. **TrendTrack** already archives them for many brands.

## Step 4: the traffic (5 minutes)

**Similarweb** gives you a ballpark of the traffic and its breakdown: search, social, direct.

## Step 5: the summary (5 minutes)

| Question | Where to find the answer |
| --- | --- |
| Which product drives the brand? | Featured products + longest-running ads |
| How does it acquire customers? | Traffic sources + ad volume |
| How does it bring them back? | Email flows + loyalty program |
