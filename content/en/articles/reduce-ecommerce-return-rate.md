## Before the purchase

1. A size guide with real measurements, not size conversions.
2. Photos of the product being worn, from several angles.
3. Customer reviews with photos, for example with **Loox**.

## After the purchase

4. Offer an exchange first, with a tool like **Loop** or **ReturnGO**.
5. Offer a bonus on store credit instead of a refund.
6. Analyze return reasons every month, product by product.
