Rates checked on Nov 20, 2026 on the [La Poste Pro Colissimo page](https://www.laposte.fr/professionnel/tarif-colissimo) and the [La Poste rates page for shipping abroad](https://www.laposte.fr/tarifs-postaux-etranger) (both in French), from mainland France. The EU and Switzerland zone also includes Liechtenstein, San Marino and the Vatican. The pages do not say whether these amounts include VAT.

The two pages do not present the 20 kg and 30 kg bands the same way: for those weights, check the grid directly when you ship.

## What about outside the European Union?

La Poste groups the other destinations into two zones. Zone B covers non-EU Eastern Europe (excluding Switzerland and Russia), Norway and the Maghreb. Zone C covers the other destinations, including Africa outside the Maghreb, Canada, the United States, Russia and the Near and Middle East.

## How much does Europe cost compared with France?

About twice as much. A 1 kg parcel costs €9.59 to mainland France in 2026 on the same [La Poste Pro page](https://www.laposte.fr/professionnel/tarif-colissimo), versus €19.39 to the EU. At 2 kg, it is €11.19 versus €22.19. The full France grid is in our article on [Colissimo rates 2026 for an online store](/en/shipping/colissimo-rates-online-store-2026/).

Calculation for 100 orders of 1 kg a month, based on the grids above:

| Destination | 2026 | 2027 | Difference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mainland France | €959 | €1,009 | +€50 |
| EU and Switzerland | €1,939 | €2,039 | +€100 |
| United Kingdom | €2,339 | €2,439 | +€100 |

This calculation assumes every parcel weighs 1 kg and follows the public grid. If you have a Colissimo business contract, your negotiated rates may differ.

## How much do rates rise in 2027?

To the EU and Switzerland, the increase ranges from 5.2% (1 kg) to 5.6% (5 kg) on the 500 g to 15 kg bands. To the United Kingdom it ranges from 4.2% to 5.1%, because the increase in euros is the same as to the EU (from €0.80 to €3.70 depending on weight) on a higher base.

That is more than the 4.1% average increase announced by La Poste in its [press release of July 27, 2026](https://www.caissedesdepots.fr/sites/ms-cdc-fr/files/2026-07/CP_La_Poste_tarifs_au_010127.pdf) (in French) for mainland France and international shipments. An average covers all shipments: on the bands an online store uses most, the increase is higher.

## What is included?

According to the [La Poste Pro page](https://www.laposte.fr/professionnel/tarif-colissimo), Colissimo international includes proof of drop-off, parcel tracking and delivery by hand or to the letterbox. If the recipient is absent, they receive a delivery notice.

The page states a delivery time of 3 to 8 days depending on the destination, with no commitment by country. Weight is capped at 30 kg, length + width + height at 150 cm and length at under 100 cm. It mentions neither pickup-point delivery abroad nor a compensation amount for international parcels.

## Is there anything cheaper than Colissimo to Europe?

Multi-carrier platforms show other offers. For example, Packlink shows Mondial Relay home delivery to Europe at €9.50 incl. VAT for a 0.5 kg parcel to Germany, Belgium, Luxembourg and the Netherlands, €10.20 incl. VAT to Spain and the United Kingdom, and €11.20 incl. VAT to Italy, Portugal and Austria ([Packlink's Mondial Relay Europe page](https://www.packlink.com/fr-FR/transporteurs/mondial-relay/domicile-europe/), in French, checked on Nov 20, 2026). The stated delivery time is 3 to 6 working days.

These are an intermediary's prices, not a Mondial Relay grid, and the service is not the same: compare at equivalent weight, delivery time and insurance. To choose a platform, see [Sendcloud vs Boxtal](/en/shipping/sendcloud-vs-boxtal/).

## How to choose

- You send a few parcels a month to Europe: the public Colissimo grid is enough, but build the doubled cost compared with France into your shipping fees.
- You sell regularly in Belgium, Germany or Spain: ask for a Colissimo business contract quote, which also opens the Colissimo Return grid, and compare it with a multi-carrier platform.
- You are setting your shipping fees for 2027: use the published 2027 grid (+5.2% to 5.6% to the EU), not the 4.1% average.
