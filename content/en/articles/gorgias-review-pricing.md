## What Gorgias does

According to its [Shopify App Store listing](https://apps.shopify.com/helpdesk):

- **Orders inside the ticket.** Your agents edit an order, issue a refund or cancel an order without leaving the ticket.
- **Unified inbox** for email, live chat, SMS, social and voice.
- **Automation**: rules, macros and auto-tagging for repetitive tickets.
- **AI Agent** for order, shipping and return questions, 24/7, plus an AI Shopping Assistant for product questions.
- **Analytics** for support and revenue tracking.
- **Integrations** listed: Shopify Flow, Klaviyo, Loop Returns, Recharge, Yotpo, AfterShip and Attentive.

## Gorgias pricing

| Plan | Monthly price | Annual | Tickets included | Agents | Extra tickets |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | $10 | Not offered | 50 | 3 | $0.40 per ticket |
| Basic | $60 | $600/year | 300 | Unlimited | $40 per 100 tickets |
| Pro | $360 | $3,600/year | 2,000 | Up to 500 | $36 per 100 tickets |
| Advanced | $900 | $9,000/year | 5,000 | Up to 500 | $36 per 100 tickets |

Pricing checked on the [Gorgias Shopify App Store listing](https://apps.shopify.com/helpdesk) on Oct 18, 2026, in US dollars. The [Gorgias pricing page](https://www.gorgias.com/pricing) confirms that the helpdesk scales from 50 to 5,000 tickets a month with no per-agent pricing, that AI Agent is available on every plan and paid per resolution, and that voice and SMS are add-ons priced by volume. Live chat is included in all four plans.

## What an activity spike costs

Calculated from the rates above:

- **Basic plan, normal month at 300 tickets:** $60.
- **Basic plan, sales month at 800 tickets:** $60 + 5 × $40 = $260.
- **Pro plan, month at 3,000 tickets:** $360 + 10 × $36 = $720.

If your spikes happen every month, compare with the next plan up: included quota costs less than overage tickets.

## Who it's for

- **Shopify stores** that want to handle order requests without switching between the helpdesk and Shopify admin.
- **Teams with many agents and moderate ticket volume**: on Basic, Pro and Advanced, adding an agent doesn't change the price.
- **Small stores** starting with 50 tickets a month on Starter.

## Strengths and limitations

**Strengths:** actions on Shopify orders from the ticket, billing independent of agent count (beyond Starter), every channel in one inbox, common ecommerce integrations.

**Limitations:** the bill follows your ticket volume, so it rises during sales. The app is English only. AI Agent pricing isn't shown publicly.

## The alternative

**Zendesk** bills per agent: from $19/agent/month (Support Team, annual) to $115/agent/month (Suite Professional, annual), according to its [pricing page](https://www.zendesk.com/pricing/) checked on Oct 18, 2026.
