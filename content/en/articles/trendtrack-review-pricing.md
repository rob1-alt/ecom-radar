## What TrendTrack does

**TrendTrack** bundles several tools you would usually buy separately.

### Store analysis

Search Shopify stores by niche, country or theme, open a detailed profile for each store (estimated traffic, installed apps, products) and get suggestions for similar stores. The vendor says it tracks more than 10,000 trending stores.

### Ad library

A library of Meta and TikTok ads you can filter by advertiser. TrendTrack claims 95 million indexed TikToks and 700,000 brand profiles updated every 24 hours.

### Brand emails

An email library that shows you the flows and campaigns your competitors send: welcome, abandoned cart, promotions. That's rare for this type of tool.

### BrandTracker

Track a list of brands over time: new ads, new products, store changes. The Starter plan tracks 2 brands, Pro 10 and Business 20.

### Chrome extension and AI integration

The free extension shows the traffic, theme and apps of the store you're visiting. The MCP integration lets you ask TrendTrack questions directly from Claude or ChatGPT.

## Who it's for

- **Dropshippers** looking for products and stores that work.
- **Media buyers and creative strategists** who want to see competitors' active ads.
- **DTC brands** tracking a few direct competitors with the BrandTracker.
- **Agencies** preparing competitor audits for their clients (Business plan, multiple users).

## Strengths and limitations

**Strengths:** store, ads and emails in one tool, data updated daily, free extension, MCP integration for AI assistants.

**Limitations:** traffic and sales estimates are still estimates, as with every tool in this category. Image searches are limited on the smaller plans.
