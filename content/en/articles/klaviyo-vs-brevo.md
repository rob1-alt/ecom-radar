## Two ways of billing

This is the real difference between the two tools, and it changes everything when you work out your budget.

**Klaviyo** charges by active profiles. According to its ["How Klaviyo pricing works"](https://www.klaviyo.com/platform/how-klaviyo-pricing-works) page, only reachable contacts count: unsubscribed and suppressed contacts are not billed. Each profile tier includes a monthly send allowance. On its [Shopify listing](https://apps.shopify.com/klaviyo-email-marketing), the free plan covers email for up to 250 contacts, and the paid email plan starts at $20/month for 251 to 500 contacts. Prices are in US dollars.

**Brevo** charges by the number of emails sent each month. According to its [pricing grid](https://brevo.com/pricing.md), Starter starts at €7/month, Standard at €15/month and Professional at €350/month on monthly billing, with 10% off on annual billing. Watch out: the same grid sets a maximum number of contacts for each volume tier, namely 500 contacts at the 5,000 emails/month tier, 1,500 at the 10,000 tier and up to 500,000 from 20,000 emails/month. Check your tier in the calculator before you subscribe.

In short: with a large list you email rarely, Brevo's model works in your favor. With a smaller list you email often, run the numbers on both calculators.

## Brevo's plans in detail

| Free | €0 | Up to 300 emails per day | Basic email |

| Standard | From €15/month | Depends on the tier | Marketing automation, A/B testing, send-time optimization, web tracking |

Pricing checked on [Brevo pricing page](https://www.brevo.com/pricing/) and its [detailed pricing grid](https://brevo.com/pricing.md) on Oct 18, 2026.

Key point for ecommerce: on Brevo, automated flows (welcome, abandoned cart, post-purchase) require the **Standard** plan. Starter is mainly for newsletters and one-off campaigns.

## Shopify integration

**Klaviyo** is on the Shopify App Store as "Klaviyo: Email Marketing & SMS". The [listing](https://apps.shopify.com/klaviyo-email-marketing) mentions syncing Shopify data (tags, catalog, coupons), 350+ integrations, segments and flow triggers based on historical and real-time data, and a guided setup with a sign-up form, a welcome email and an abandoned-checkout flow. The app is available in French.

**Brevo**: as of Oct 18, 2026, the old "Brevo" listing on the Shopify App Store says [the app is not available](https://apps.shopify.com/sendinblue). On Shopify, Brevo goes through [Brevo PushOwl](https://apps.shopify.com/pushowl), an email, push and SMS app. Its free plan includes 500 push notifications and from 500 emails per month, the Plus plan costs $19/month (with abandoned cart recovery) and the Power plan $79/month. The app is also available in French.

## SMS

At **Klaviyo**, SMS (like MMS, WhatsApp and RCS) is billed separately from email: you commit to a spend amount that burns down as you send, with rates that vary by country, according to the [pricing explainer page](https://www.klaviyo.com/platform/how-klaviyo-pricing-works). The [help center](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115000976672) states that every customer gets $5 of free Mobile Messaging credit each month.

At **Brevo**, SMS credits are sold separately from the email plans, according to the [pricing page](https://www.brevo.com/pricing/).

## When to choose Brevo

- You're starting out and want the lowest entry price (Starter from €7/month).
- You have a large contact list but send little: billing by email volume works in your favor.
- You also want push and WhatsApp (Professional plan, or PushOwl for push on Shopify).
- Take at least the **Standard** plan if you want automated flows.

## When to choose Klaviyo

- Your store runs on Shopify and you want to segment on synced order data, catalog and tags.
- You want a guided setup with the basic flows already suggested.
- Your list is moderate or highly engaged: you only pay for active profiles.
