## Head to head in one table

| Criterion | **Klaviyo** | **Brevo** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price at 10,000 contacts | ~$150/month | ~€65/month |
| Shopify integration | Native, very deep | Official app |
| SMS | Included, pay as you go | Included, pay as you go |
| Learning curve | Medium | Easy |

## When to choose Brevo

**Brevo** fits stores that are setting up their first flows (welcome, abandoned cart, post-purchase) and want a French tool that is simple and cheap.

## When to choose Klaviyo

**Klaviyo** becomes the best choice when you segment on purchase history, margin or likelihood to buy again, and every point of conversion rate counts.
