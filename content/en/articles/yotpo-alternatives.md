## Yotpo at a glance

According to its [Shopify App Store listing](https://apps.shopify.com/yotpo-social-reviews), **Yotpo** Product Reviews comes in three plans:

| Plan | Price | Orders | What it adds |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | Up to 50 per month | Automatic review requests, email template library, sentiment and profanity check, on-site review display |
| Starter | $15/month or $144/year | Pricing changes with order volume | Photos and videos, Google rich snippets, Google Shopping Ads, reviews carousel |
| Pro | $119/month or $1,068/year | Pricing changes with order volume | Google Seller Rating, custom questions, AI reviews summary, smart sorting, technical support by email |

Pricing checked on the [Yotpo Shopify App Store listing](https://apps.shopify.com/yotpo-social-reviews) on Oct 18, 2026. Prices in USD; external charges may be billed by Yotpo outside your Shopify invoice, according to the listing.

The main reason to look elsewhere: paid plan pricing changes with your order volume, and Google Seller Rating and the AI summary are limited to the Pro plan.

## The alternatives

| Alternative | Free plan | First paid plan | Top plan |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Judge.me** | Yes, unlimited review requests | Awesome, $15/month at most | - |
| **Loox** | Up to 500 orders | Convert, $49.99/month (300 orders included, +$50 per extra 300) | Unlimited, $299.99/month |
| **Okendo** | Up to 50 orders/month | Essential, $19/month (200 orders) | Power, $299/month (3,500 orders) |

Pricing checked on Oct 18, 2026 on the [Judge.me pricing page](https://judge.me/pricing), the [Loox Shopify App Store listing](https://apps.shopify.com/loox) and the [Okendo Shopify App Store listing](https://apps.shopify.com/okendo-reviews). Prices in USD.

## The alternatives in detail

### Judge.me

The free plan includes unlimited review requests, then the Awesome plan costs $15/month at most, according to the [Judge.me pricing page](https://judge.me/pricing). That is the lowest price ceiling of the four tools compared here. Detailed comparison with Loox in our [Judge.me vs Loox](/en/reviews/judge-me-vs-loox/) head-to-head.

### Loox

According to its [Shopify listing](https://apps.shopify.com/loox), the Free plan covers up to 500 orders with review request emails and reminders, photo-review discounts, 17+ widgets, SEO rich snippets and 24/7 chat and email support. Convert ($49.99/month, 300 orders included then $50 per extra 300) adds video reviews, AI sorting and summaries, AI translations, referrals and Google, Meta and TikTok syndication. Unlimited costs $299.99/month. Paid plans have a 14-day free trial.

### Okendo

The Reviews module is free up to 50 orders per month, then Essential at $19/month (200 orders), Growth at $119/month (1,500 orders) and Power at $299/month (3,500 orders), according to [Okendo's Shopify listing](https://apps.shopify.com/okendo-reviews). Okendo also sells loyalty, referrals, quizzes and surveys, on quote. Full details in our [Okendo review](/en/reviews/okendo-review-pricing/).

## And for brand reviews

If you mostly want reviews about your company rather than your products, **Trustpilot** has a free plan with 50 invitations per month and paid plans from $99/month billed annually, and **Avis Vérifiés** starts at €35/month for 200 orders, according to the pages checked on Oct 18, 2026. Details in our [Trustpilot vs Avis Vérifiés](/en/reviews/trustpilot-vs-avis-verifies/) head-to-head.

## How to choose

- **Tight budget, you want to pay as little as possible**: **Judge.me**, capped at $15/month.
- **You're starting out and want a broad free plan**: **Loox**, free up to 500 orders.
- **You want reviews, loyalty and referrals from one vendor**: **Okendo**, or stay on **Yotpo**.
