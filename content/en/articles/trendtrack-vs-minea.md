## Head to head

| Criterion | **TrendTrack** | **Minea** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stores | Shopify store search + detailed store profile | Store listings |
| Ads | Meta and TikTok | Meta (TikTok, Pinterest, Snapchat "coming soon") |
| Products | Through store analysis | Product listings + daily niche lists |
| Brand emails | Yes | Not mentioned |
| Brand tracking | BrandTracker: 2, 10 or 20 brands depending on the plan | Brand tracker included in all 3 plans |
| AI features | MCP integration (Claude, ChatGPT) | AI ad analysis, AI transcript, Magic search |
| Chrome extension | Free | Yes |
| Starting price | €34/month (annual) | €49/month (monthly), €39/month (quarterly) |

Sources: [TrendTrack pricing page](https://www.trendtrack.io/fr/pricing), [Minea pricing page](https://www.minea.com/fr/pricing) and [Minea website](https://www.minea.com/fr), checked on Oct 18, 2026.

## Two different approaches

**TrendTrack** starts from the brand. You pick a competitor and see its store (estimated traffic, apps, products), its Meta and TikTok ads and its emails, then follow it over time with the BrandTracker. The vendor claims 95 million indexed TikToks and 700,000 brand profiles updated every 24 hours. Details are in our [TrendTrack review](/en/competitor-intel/trendtrack-review-pricing/).

**Minea** starts from search. Its plans list Meta ads, products and stores, with daily niche lists and notifications. Minea claims more than 80 million ads and stores, 120,000 updates per day and more than 200,000 dropshippers using it, according to [its homepage](https://www.minea.com/fr). Snapchat, Pinterest and TikTok ads are shown as "coming soon" on the pricing page as of Oct 18, 2026.

**What this means at entry level:** TrendTrack Starter costs €34/month if you commit for a year. Minea Starter costs €39/month if you pay quarterly, €49/month with no commitment.

## When to choose TrendTrack

- You're a **DTC brand or an agency** and want to understand what a few specific competitors are doing.
- You want to see **their emails** (welcome, abandoned cart, promos): the feature Minea does not mention.
- You want **TikTok ads** today, not just Meta.
- You want to query the data from **Claude or ChatGPT** through the MCP integration.

## When to choose Minea

- You do **dropshipping** and look for winning products at scale from Meta ads.
- You want **daily niche lists** and notifications without building your own monitoring.
- You want **built-in AI features** to break down an ad (analysis, transcript, Magic search).
- You prefer paying **monthly or quarterly** rather than yearly.

## What to keep in mind

Both tools show **estimates** (traffic, sales, ad performance), not stores' real figures. The announced volumes (95 million TikToks for TrendTrack, 80 million ads and stores for Minea) are vendor figures, not independently verified. Also check Minea's TikTok coverage when you subscribe, since it is shown as upcoming.
