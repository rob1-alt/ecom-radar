## Head to head

| Criterion | **TrendTrack** | **Minea** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stores | Shopify store search + detailed store profile | Store listings |
| Ads | Meta, TikTok (ads and organic) and Google | Meta (TikTok, Pinterest, Snapchat "coming soon" in the plan text) |
| Products | Through store analysis | Product listings + daily niche lists |
| Brand emails | Yes (Email Library) | Not mentioned |
| Brand tracking | BrandTracker: 2, 10 or 20 brands depending on the plan | Brand tracker included in all 3 plans (unlimited on Premium and Business) |
| AI features | MCP integration (Claude, ChatGPT) | AI ad analysis, AI transcript, Magic search |
| Starting price | €34/month (annual) | €49/month (monthly), €39/month (quarterly) |

Sources: [TrendTrack pricing page](https://www.trendtrack.io/fr/pricing), [Minea pricing page](https://www.minea.com/fr/pricing) and [Minea website](https://www.minea.com/fr), checked on Nov 22, 2026 (first checked on Oct 18, 2026: prices unchanged).

## Two different approaches

**TrendTrack** starts from the brand. You pick a competitor and see its store (estimated traffic, apps, products), its Meta and TikTok ads and its emails, then follow it over time with the BrandTracker. Its pricing page states data refreshed every 24 hours and 2 to 3 million new ads indexed every 24 hours. Details are in our [TrendTrack review](/en/competitor-intel/trendtrack-review-pricing/).

**Minea** starts from search. Its plans list Meta ads, products and stores, with daily niche lists and notifications. Minea claims more than 80 million ads and stores, 120,000 updates per day and more than 200,000 dropshippers using it, according to [its homepage](https://www.minea.com/fr). As of Nov 22, 2026, the plan text still says "Snapchat, Pinterest, TikTok ads (coming soon)", while the comparison table on the same page lists a TikTok ad listing row.

## Pricing compared

| Starter | €34/month | 2 | 5 per week | 20 flows, 3 campaigns |

Pricing checked on [TrendTrack pricing page](https://www.trendtrack.io/fr/pricing) on Nov 22, 2026 (same prices as on Oct 18). Annual billing gives -30%, quarterly -15%. Business includes 3 users, then $29 per extra user. On Nov 22, a Black Friday banner announced "up to 50% off annual plans" for a limited time, so the price you pay may be lower than the table during the offer.

### Minea

| Plan | Monthly | Quarterly (per month) | AI ad analysis | AI transcript | Magic search |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | €49/month | €39/month | 10 per month | 10 per month | 50 per month |
| Premium | €99/month | €79/month | 50 per month | 50 per month | 100 per month |
| Business | €199/month | €158/month | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |

Pricing checked on [Minea pricing page](https://www.minea.com/fr/pricing) on Nov 22, 2026, same as on Oct 18. All three plans include Meta ad, product and store listings, the Brand tracker, notifications, daily niche lists and a free .Store domain. Premium and Business make the Brand tracker unlimited; notifications are limited to 10 stores on Premium and unlimited on Business. An annual tab exists but no annual price is shown in plain text. The [English version of the page](https://www.minea.com/pricing) shows the same amounts in dollars ($49, $99, $199).

**What this means at entry level:** TrendTrack Starter costs €34/month if you commit for a year. Minea Starter costs €39/month if you pay quarterly, €49/month with no commitment.

## When to choose TrendTrack

- You're a **DTC brand or an agency** and want to understand what a few specific competitors are doing.
- You want to see **their emails** (welcome, abandoned cart, promos): the feature Minea does not mention.
- You want **TikTok and Google ads** today, not just Meta.
- You want to query the data from **Claude or ChatGPT** through the MCP integration.

## When to choose Minea

- You do **dropshipping** and look for winning products at scale from Meta ads.
- You want **daily niche lists** and notifications without building your own monitoring.
- You want **built-in AI features** to break down an ad (analysis, transcript, Magic search).
- You prefer paying **monthly or quarterly** rather than yearly.

## What to keep in mind

Both tools show **estimates** (traffic, sales, ad performance), not stores' real figures. The announced volumes (2 to 3 million new ads per day for TrendTrack, more than 80 million ads and stores for Minea) are vendor figures, not independently verified. Also check Minea's TikTok coverage when you subscribe, since its pricing page contradicts itself on this point.
