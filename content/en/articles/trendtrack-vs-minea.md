## Head to head

| Criterion | **TrendTrack** | **Minea** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shopify stores | Search + detailed analysis | Store analysis |
| Ads | Meta and TikTok | Facebook, TikTok, Pinterest, Snapchat |
| Brand emails | Yes | No |
| Brand tracking | BrandTracker (2 to 20 brands) | Limited |
| Chrome extension | Free | Yes |
| AI integration (MCP) | Yes | Not disclosed |
| Starting price | €34/month (annual) | See the vendor's website |

## When to choose TrendTrack

You're a brand or an agency and you want to understand what a few specific competitors are doing: their new ads, their products, their emails. The BrandTracker and the email library make the difference.

## When to choose Minea

You do dropshipping and you test a lot of products. Coverage of Pinterest and Snapchat widens your search for winning products.
