| Stars | Where they show | Threshold stated by Google | How to get them |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Product stars (Product Ratings) | Shopping ads and free Google Shopping listings | 50 product reviews across all products | Approved aggregator or XML data source in Merchant Center, updated at least once a month |
| Store ratings | Search and Shopping ads, Google Customer Reviews badge | Generally 100 eligible, unique reviews; 3.5/5 minimum rating in ads | Google Customer Reviews or a recognized review partner, calculated per country |
| Rich snippets | Google organic results | No threshold on the pages checked | Review markup on your product pages (included in the free **Judge.me** plan, for example) |

Sources: Google Merchant Center help on [product ratings](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/14549080?hl=en), [submitting product reviews](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/7045996?hl=en-GB) and [store ratings](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/190657), checked on Nov 21, 2026.

## How many reviews do you need for product stars?

Google is clear: you need **at least 50 product reviews across your whole catalog**. "If you have less than 50 reviews, your product ratings will not show on Google Shopping or Google Ads," according to the [eligibility page](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/14549080?hl=en).

Other conditions Google lists:

- a Merchant Center account linked to your verified website, standalone or a sub-account (not a multi-client account);
- product URLs on the same domain as the one registered in Merchant Center;
- reviews matched to products by GTIN, or by brand and manufacturer part number (MPN);
- a full data source sent **at least once a month** to stay eligible.

Allow **2 to 4 weeks** between the initial submission and the stars appearing, according to Google. Google also filters reviews and only shows ratings when it judges them accurate and relevant.

## Two ways to send your reviews to Google

1. **Use an approved aggregator.** Your review app sends the data to Google for you. Google lists Judge.me, Loox, Okendo, Yotpo, Trustpilot and Verified Reviews (Avis Vérifiés) by Skeepers, as well as Bazaarvoice, eKomi, Feefo, Junip, PowerReviews, Reviews.io, Stamped, Trusted Shops and Guaranteed Reviews Company, according to its [help page](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/7045996?hl=en-GB).
2. **Upload your own XML data source** in Merchant Center (Reviews menu, then join product ratings). Upload by scheduled fetch, SFTP, Google Cloud Storage, file or API. The help pages checked don't mention a fee, but you need to collect reviews elsewhere and generate the file every month.

Note: if Google detects that an approved aggregator already sends your reviews, the direct sign-up button is disabled.

## What about store ratings?

Store ratings (formerly seller ratings) rate your store, not a product. According to the [Merchant Center page](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/190657):

- you **generally need at least 100 eligible, unique reviews**, assessed over a rolling 24-month window (the [Google Customer Reviews FAQ](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/14632922) refers to the last 12 months instead: Google's pages aren't aligned);
- the rating is **calculated separately for each country**: reviews from French customers count for France;
- you need a rating of at least **3.5 out of 5** for it to show in ads.

The direct route is **Google Customer Reviews**, available in France according to [Google's FAQ](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/14632922). You show a survey opt-in after checkout, and Google sends the customer a one-question survey a few days after the estimated delivery date. Those answers are used to calculate your store rating. Reviews from recognized partners count too: Trustpilot, Yotpo, Okendo, Reviews.io, Trusted Shops, Feefo and Verified Reviews were on the list checked on Nov 21, 2026. Judge.me and Loox were not.

## How to choose

- **Small budget, Shopify store:** **Judge.me** Awesome at $15/month. The free plan isn't enough for Google Shopping, but it already gives you rich snippets in organic results. 15-day trial on Awesome.
- **Photo and video reviews, plus Meta and TikTok syndication:** **Loox** Convert, budgeting for order blocks. Compare in our [Judge.me vs Loox](/en/reviews/judge-me-vs-loox/) head-to-head.
- **Reviews, loyalty and referrals in one tool:** **Okendo** from Growth. Details in our [Okendo review](/en/reviews/okendo-review-pricing/).
- **Certified purchase-linked reviews, French store not on Shopify:** **Avis Vérifiés**, which includes Google Shopping stars from Starter. See [Trustpilot vs Avis Vérifiés](/en/reviews/trustpilot-vs-avis-verifies/).
- **Your reviews already live in an in-house CMS:** the direct XML data source in Merchant Center, if you can regenerate it every month.

Either way, collect your first 50 product reviews first: without them, no tool will make the stars appear.
