## App by app

### 1. Judge.me

**Judge.me** has the most generous free plan in the category. According to its [Shopify listing](https://apps.shopify.com/judgeme), the Forever Free plan includes unlimited product and store reviews, unlimited photo and video reviews, the review widget and star rating badge, carousels, Google rich snippets sync, syndication to Shop App, Etsy and Amazon, and a reviews importer.

The Awesome plan at $15/month (15-day trial) adds AI features (replies, summaries, translations), 130+ integrations, advanced collection with auto-reminders, coupons and referrals, and Google Shopping, Meta and TikTok Shop sync. The app has the Built for Shopify badge, has 48,701 reviews on the Shopify App Store and is available in French.

### 2. Avis Vérifiés

**Avis Vérifiés** is Skeepers' review solution. According to [its website](https://fr.avis-verifies.com/), review collection, management and moderation are certified NF ISO 20488 by AFNOR, and the vendor presents itself as a Google Review Partner, with reviews feeding Google Seller Ratings. It connects to Shopify, PrestaShop, Magento and WooCommerce. It collects product, brand and store reviews, and adds NPS, CSAT and CES surveys. There is no public price: pricing depends on review volume, on quote.

### 3. Loox

**Loox** focuses on photo reviews. On its [Shopify listing](https://apps.shopify.com/loox), the Free plan covers up to 500 orders, with review request emails and reminders, photo-review discounts, 17+ widgets and rich snippets. The Convert plan at $49.99/month includes 300 orders (then $50 per additional 300 orders) and adds video reviews, AI features, referrals and Google, Meta and TikTok syndication. The Unlimited plan costs $299.99/month. The app has the Built for Shopify badge but is available in English only.

### 4. Okendo

**Okendo** combines reviews and loyalty. On its [Shopify listing](https://apps.shopify.com/okendo-reviews), billing follows order volume: Free up to 50 orders/month, Essential at $19/month up to 200, Growth at $119/month up to 1,500 (with AI summaries, a Q&A widget and TikTok Shop integration) and Power at $299/month up to 3,500. Paid plans include a 14-day trial. The app is listed as English-only.

### 5. Trustpilot

**Trustpilot** is mainly for brand reviews. On its [pricing page](https://business.trustpilot.com/pricing), the Free plan includes 50 review invitations per month, Starter starts at $99/month (100 invitations), Plus at $319/month (300 invitations) and Premium at $799/month (1,000 invitations), per domain and billed annually, with a 12-month commitment. Enterprise is custom-priced.

### 6. Yotpo

**Yotpo** offers a free plan up to 50 orders/month according to its [Shopify listing](https://apps.shopify.com/yotpo-social-reviews). Starter at $15/month adds photo and video collection, Google rich snippets and Google Shopping Ads. Pro at $119/month adds Google seller ratings, custom questions and an AI review summary. Paid plan prices vary with order volume, and Yotpo may bill some charges outside the Shopify invoice. The app is listed in English.
