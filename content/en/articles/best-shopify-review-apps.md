## App by app

### 1. Judge.me

**Judge.me** is the most installed app in the category. The free plan covers the essentials, and the paid plan adds photo reviews, Q&A and imports from other apps.

### 2. Avis Vérifiés

**Avis Vérifiés** is the standard for French brands. Its reviews are certified and show up in Google Shopping, which matters if you run paid acquisition.

### 3. Loox

**Loox** goes all in on photo and video reviews. It's the right choice for fashion, beauty or home decor, where seeing the product worn or in place drives sales.

## What AI assistants say

Across the 18 "Shopify customer reviews" prompts we tracked in October, **Judge.me** appears in 41% of ChatGPT answers, versus 28% for **Yotpo**, which is down 6 points in a month.

## Our method

Each app is installed on a test store, with the same 50 orders. We score setup, mobile rendering, impact on page speed and the real price at 1,000 orders a month.
