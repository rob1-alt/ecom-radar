## Two approaches to customer reviews

**Trustpilot** is first a platform for reviews about the company. You invite customers after an order (by email or automation, according to [Trustpilot's Shopify app](https://apps.shopify.com/trustpilot-reviews)), and reviews appear on your Trustpilot page and in TrustBox widgets on your store. Product reviews exist, but as a **paid add-on** from the Starter plan.

**Avis Vérifiés**, published by Skeepers, collects brand, product and store reviews **tied to proof of purchase**. The website announces collection, management and moderation certified **NF ISO 20488 (AFNOR)**, more than 8,000 client brands in Europe and more than 26 million reviews, according to [its homepage](https://fr.avis-verifies.com/).

**What this means for a small store:** Trustpilot Starter comes to $1,188 per year for 100 invitations per month, with no product reviews included. Avis Vérifiés Starter starts at €69/month for 400 orders, with Google Shopping stars included. If you just want to get started, Trustpilot's Free plan (50 invitations per month) costs nothing.

## Google and star ratings

This is often the deciding factor for a store.

- **Avis Vérifiés** presents itself as a **Google Review Partner**. The Starter plan includes stars in Google results (rich snippets and Google Shopping) and the **Google Seller Ratings** partnership.
- **Trustpilot** describes itself as a Google-approved review platform on [its blog](https://fi.business.trustpilot.com/blog/get-seen-in-search/how-your-ecommerce-business-can-benefit-from-stars-in-search). The same article recalls Google's thresholds: most merchants earn Seller Ratings after about 100 eligible reviews over 12 months, and Google Shopping product stars require at least 50 verified post-purchase product reviews. On Trustpilot, those product reviews go through the paid add-on.

## When to choose Avis Vérifiés

- You sell mainly in **France or Europe** and want reviews certified **NF ISO 20488** and tied to proof of purchase.
- You want **Google Shopping stars** on your products without paying for a separate option.
- You also run **physical stores** (in-store collection on Advanced).

## When to choose Trustpilot

- You want to **start for free** with 50 invitations per month.
- What matters to you is **company reputation** on a public page, more than product reviews.
