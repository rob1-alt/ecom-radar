## Head to head

| Criterion | **Trustpilot** | **Avis Vérifiés** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Review type | Brand reviews | Product and brand reviews |
| Certification | Open to anyone | Reviews tied to an order |
| Google Shopping stars | Yes | Yes |
| Read by AI assistants | Very often | Often |

## Why Trustpilot carries weight with AI assistants

Trustpilot pages are public, rank well and are often cited by ChatGPT and Perplexity when you ask them whether a store is trustworthy.
