## What Omnisend does

According to the [Omnisend Shopify App Store listing](https://apps.shopify.com/omnisend), the app includes:

- **Email and SMS campaigns** built from 250+ email templates.
- **Ready-made automations**: abandoned cart, welcome, post-purchase.
- **Forms and popups**, including exit-intent.
- **Segmentation** and dynamic content personalization.
- **Web push**, included from the free plan.
- **AI** for product recommendations, subject lines and copy.
- **MCP connection** to build campaigns and segments from ChatGPT or Claude.

For integrations, the listing includes Checkout, Shopify Flow, Aftership, Gorgias, Justuno, Loox, Recharge and Yotpo. The app is in English, with support also offered in French, Spanish, Italian, German and Chinese.

Pricing checked on the [Omnisend pricing page](https://www.omnisend.com/pricing/) on Oct 18, 2026, cross-checked with the [Shopify App Store listing](https://apps.shopify.com/omnisend). Prices in US dollars excluding tax, rising with the size of your list.

What to know before choosing:

- **What you're billed for**: subscribers and non-subscribers who received automated messages. Unsubscribed contacts aren't billed.
- **Discount**: paying 3 months upfront saves 30% ($33.60 instead of $48 for Standard, $123.90 instead of $177 for Pro), then monthly billing.
- **Branding**: required on the free plan, optional on Standard and Pro.
- **US SMS**: $0.009 per SMS for $10 to $49.99 purchased, $0.0085 up to $999.99, $0.008 up to $9,999.99 and $0.007 above. Price varies by country.
- **Account support**: a free account expert from $400/month, and the Custom plan adds priority support, advanced reporting and free migration in up to 5 days.

## Who it's for

- **Shopify stores just starting out**: the free plan has no time limit and already includes web push.
- **Brands that want email and SMS in one place**: one dashboard, with pay-as-you-go SMS on the Pro plan.
- **Large lists (150,000+ active profiles)**: quote-based Custom plan.

## Strengths and limitations

**Strengths:** free plan with no time limit, web push included, ready-made e-commerce automations, 24/7 support, 30% off when paying 3 months upfront.

**Limitations:** the Standard plan caps at 6,000 emails a month and SMS pricing is only detailed on Pro, the price rises with your contact count and the pricing page doesn't show each tier's price: simulate your volume directly on it.
