At **Judge.me**, the pricing page states it plainly: $15/month is the most you will ever pay. At **Loox**, the Convert plan rises with your orders, and the Unlimited plan becomes the better deal from about 1,800 orders.

## What sets them apart

**Judge.me** puts a lot into its free plan: unlimited reviews, photo and video, unlimited review requests, Google rich snippets, carousels, 24/7 chat and email support, SOC 2 Type 2 and ISO 27001 compliance. The Awesome plan adds AI, social media and Google Shopping, referrals and coupons, all integrations, full widget and email customization, and automatic review reminders, according to its [pricing page](https://judge.me/pricing).

**Loox** is built around photo and video reviews: according to its [Shopify App Store listing](https://apps.shopify.com/loox), it collects them through automated emails, QR codes and discounts, syncs reviews with Shop App, Meta Shops, Google Shopping and TikTok Shop, turns reviews into social posts with Loox Studio and offers referral rewards. Widgets and emails can be translated into 38 languages.

## Which one to choose

- **Tight budget or high order volume:** **Judge.me**, capped at $15/month.
- **Under 500 orders and want to try photo reviews with discounts:** both free plans work.
- **Video reviews, Meta and TikTok syndication, social posts from reviews:** **Loox** Convert, budgeting for the order blocks.
