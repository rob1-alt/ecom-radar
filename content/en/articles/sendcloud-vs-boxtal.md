## Sendcloud pricing

| Free | €0 | 600 shipments/year | Labels paid individually | 1 | 2 max. |
| Lite | €26/month | 400/month | €0.11 | 3 | 3 max. |

| Premium | €164/month | 10,000/month | €0.09 | 10 | Unlimited |
| Pro | €599/month | 30,000/month | €0.08 | Unlimited | Unlimited |

Pricing checked on the [Sendcloud pricing page](https://www.sendcloud.com/fr/tarifs/) on Oct 18, 2026. Above the monthly quota, each label costs €0.15 more (€0.26 on Lite). The page offers 25% off with annual billing but doesn't say whether the displayed prices are monthly or annual billing: check when you sign up. The Pro plan goes through a sales expert. Shipping costs come on top in every case.

### What it costs at your volume

Calculated from the displayed prices, excluding shipping costs:

| Monthly volume | Sendcloud plan | Calculation | Software cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 300 labels | Lite | €26 + 300 × €0.11 | €59/month |
| 800 labels | Lite (overage) | €26 + 400 × €0.11 + 400 × €0.26 | €174/month |
| 800 labels | Growth | €89 + 800 × €0.10 | €169/month |
| 3,000 labels | Premium | €164 + 3,000 × €0.09 | €434/month |

At 800 labels a month, Growth is already cheaper than Lite with overage, and it adds the returns portal and the custom tracking page.

On the **Boxtal** side, the Shopify listing shows no subscription: the app is free and you pay each shipment at the chosen carrier's rate. The [Boxtal website](https://www.boxtal.com/fr/fr/) also offers insurance from €0.85 per shipment and parcel pickup from your premises, with no commitment.

## Sendcloud in detail

**Sendcloud** is shipping software: you connect your store, print labels with 170+ carriers and automate with shipping rules. According to the [pricing page](https://www.sendcloud.com/fr/tarifs/):

- **Free**: to get started, with Sendcloud's negotiated rates only and limited features.
- **Lite**: your own carrier contracts, tracking notifications by email, SMS and WhatsApp in 2 languages.
- **Growth**: custom tracking page with promotions, custom returns portal, notifications in 8 languages, automatic review requests, phone support.
- **Premium**: advanced returns management, tracking page analytics.
- **Pro**: everything in Premium, dedicated account manager.

## Boxtal in detail

**Boxtal** is a French multi-carrier shipping platform, based in Paris according to its [Shopify listing](https://apps.shopify.com/boxtal?locale=fr). It says it negotiates rates using the volume of its 30,000 active customers ([Boxtal website](https://www.boxtal.com/fr/fr/)). What the app and website highlight:

- **Shipping hub**: connectors import orders from your store and marketplaces into one place.
- **Labels**: creation, bulk printing, packing slips, return labels, insurance.
- **Automation**: shipping rules, carrier selection, parcel dimensions.
- **Unified tracking**: real-time tracking and email notifications.
- **Multi-carrier pickup point map** (Mondial Relay, Relais Colis, Chronopost, UPS Access Point, Colis Privé, DHL) to embed in your store.
- **Pickup** of parcels from your premises, whatever the carrier.

The app is available in French and English.

## Which one to choose

- **You're starting out or ship little**: Boxtal lets you ship with no subscription, at negotiated rates, with a pickup point map. Sendcloud's Free plan is also an option, limited to Sendcloud rates and 600 shipments a year.
- **You already have carrier contracts**: Sendcloud accepts them from the Lite plan.
- **You want branded tracking and a returns portal**: Sendcloud from Growth.
- **You also sell on French marketplaces** (Cdiscount, ManoMano, Leroy Merlin, Darty…): Boxtal lists these connectors on its [e-merchant services page](https://www.boxtal.com/fr/fr/services-ecommercants).
