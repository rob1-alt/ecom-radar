## The details

### 1. TrendTrack

**TrendTrack** combines store search, a Meta and TikTok ad library, an email library and brand tracking. It's the most complete tool for understanding a competitor's strategy, starting at €34/month. See our [TrendTrack review](/en/competitor-intel/trendtrack-review-pricing/).

### 2. Minea

**Minea** analyzes ads on Facebook, TikTok, Pinterest and Snapchat and helps you spot products that are taking off. Search volume is limited by monthly credits.

### 3. Koala Inspector

**Koala Inspector** is a Chrome extension: on a Shopify store, it shows the theme, the apps, the best-selling products and a revenue estimate. No ad data.

### 4. Similarweb

**Similarweb** estimates the traffic of any website, its sources and its audience by country. The data is less accurate for small stores.

### 5. Commerce Inspector

**Commerce Inspector** tracks a store's products and sends alerts when it launches new ones. It doesn't cover ads.

## How to choose

- You want to know everything about 2 to 10 direct competitors: **TrendTrack**.
- You're looking for winning products to test: **Minea** or **TrendTrack**.
- You just want to look at a store from time to time: **Koala Inspector**.
- You're comparing sites outside Shopify: **Similarweb**.
