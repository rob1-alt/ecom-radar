## The details

### TrendTrack

**TrendTrack** combines Shopify store search, a Meta and TikTok ad library, a brand email library and a BrandTracker that follows 2 brands on Starter, 10 on Pro and 20 on Business. Prices range from €34/month (Starter) to €111/month (Business) billed annually, with a custom Enterprise plan. The Chrome extension is free, and an MCP integration lets you query the data from Claude or ChatGPT. Full details in our [TrendTrack review](/en/competitor-intel/trendtrack-review-pricing/).

### Minea

According to its [pricing page](https://www.minea.com/pricing), **Minea** offers three plans:

- **Starter**: $49/month ($39 quarterly), 10 ad analyses and 10 transcripts a month, 50 "Magic search" queries.
- **Premium**: $99/month ($79 quarterly), 50 analyses and transcripts, 100 searches, 10 tracked shops.
- **Business**: $199/month ($158 quarterly), unlimited usage.

All plans include Meta ads, products and shops listings, a brand tracker and daily niche lists. Snapchat, Pinterest and TikTok ads were marked "coming soon" on the page when we checked: if you mostly work on TikTok, check before subscribing. For a head-to-head, read [TrendTrack vs Minea](/en/competitor-intel/trendtrack-vs-minea/).

### Koala Inspector

**Koala Inspector** is a Chrome extension built for Shopify. On a store, it shows best sellers (ranked by estimated sales volume), the theme, installed apps, a traffic estimate and the Facebook and Google campaigns driving it, according to the [vendor's website](https://koala-apps.io). The free plan never expires and covers product browsing, theme detection and app identification. The $22/month paid plan unlocks detailed sales estimates and traffic data; token packs start at $3.99.

### Similarweb

**Similarweb** estimates the traffic of any website, Shopify or not, with country-level data (100+ countries) and up to 37 months of history. The [pricing page](https://www.similarweb.com/corp/pricing/) shows a Web Intelligence offer from $125/month, custom packages for businesses, limited free data on similarweb.com and a free Chrome extension.

## How to choose

- You want to follow a few direct competitors closely (store, ads, emails): **TrendTrack**, whose Starter plan tracks 2 brands and Pro 10.
- You're mainly looking for Meta ads and products to test: **Minea** or **TrendTrack**.
- You just want to look at a store from time to time, for free: **Koala Inspector**.
- You're comparing non-Shopify sites or doing market research: **Similarweb**.

Whatever the tool, traffic and sales figures are estimates. Use them to compare stores with each other, not as real revenue.
