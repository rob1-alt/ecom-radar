## How Brevo calculates the price

The price has two parts: a base fee tied to the plan, and an email credit tier based on your monthly volume. Brevo's calculator confirms it: on Starter, 5,000 emails a month cost €7 (base fee only) and 10,000 emails cost €14, that is €7 base plus €7 for the tier.

This is a structural difference from [Klaviyo](/en/email-marketing/klaviyo-cost-25000-contacts/), which bills the number of active contacts. With Brevo, a big list you rarely email costs less than a small list you email often.

Pro shows €499 because its starting price already includes the first tier of 150,000 emails a month. SMS credits are sold separately, by volume and message destination.

## Limits to know before choosing

- **Contacts capped by tier**: 500 contacts at 5,000 emails a month, 1,500 at 10,000, 2,500 at 15,000, then 500,000 from the next tier.
- **No automations in Starter**: abandoned cart, welcome and post-purchase flows need the Standard plan.
- **Brevo logo**: removable on Starter for €9/month extra, included in Standard.
- **Shopify**: according to our [Klaviyo vs Brevo comparison](/en/email-marketing/klaviyo-vs-brevo/), the Shopify integration goes through the Brevo PushOwl app (checked on Oct 18, 2026; recheck on the Shopify App Store before subscribing).
- **No hidden commitment**: the pricing page says you choose monthly or annual commitment and that there are no hidden fees.

## Who it's for

- **Small store just starting**: the free plan (300 emails a day) lets you launch campaigns without a credit card.
- **Store with a big, lightly emailed list**: volume-based billing works in your favor.
- **Store that wants flows**: plan for Standard right away, Starter has no automation.
- **Team that wants WhatsApp, pop-ups or several users**: that's the Pro plan, from €499.
