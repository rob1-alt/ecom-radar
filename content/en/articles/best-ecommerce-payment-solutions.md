Fees vary by country and card type: check each solution's pricing.

## How to choose

### You're on Shopify

**Shopify Payments** avoids Shopify's extra transaction fees. Add **PayPal** and an installment payment solution.

### You have a custom-built store

**Stripe** has the most complete API. **Mollie** is easier to set up and offers local European payment methods.

### Your average order value is above €150

Installment payments with **Alma** or **Klarna** increase the conversion rate on high-value carts.
