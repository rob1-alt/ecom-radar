## Solution by solution

### Stripe

According to its [pricing page](https://stripe.com/fr/pricing), **Stripe** charges no setup fees and no monthly fees. Standard EEA cards cost 1.5% + €0.25, premium EEA cards 2.8% + €0.25, UK cards 2.5% + €0.25 and international cards 3.15% + €0.25, plus 2% for currency conversion. SEPA Direct Debit costs €0.35 and a dispute €20. Standard payouts are free. Klarna is available from 4.99% + €0.40.

### Shopify Payments

**Shopify Payments** is Shopify's built-in payment provider. According to [Shopify's help page for France](https://help.shopify.com/fr/manual/payments/shopify-payments/supported-countries/france), it accepts credit and debit cards, accelerated checkouts and local payment methods, its rates depend on your Shopify plan, and it is only available in mainland France (not in the overseas territories). The [pricing page](https://www.shopify.com/fr/tarifs) lists third-party transaction fees when you use another provider: 2% (Basic), 1% (Grow), 0.6% (Advanced) and 0.2% (Plus). When we checked, that page showed prices and rates in US dollars: check the euro rates in your Shopify admin.

### Mollie

[Mollie's pricing](https://www.mollie.com/fr/pricing) for France: 1.80% + €0.25 for an EEA consumer Visa or Mastercard, 2.90% + €0.25 for a business card, 3.25% + €0.25 outside the EEA, and 1.20% + €0.25 for a consumer Cartes Bancaires card. SEPA Direct Debit costs €0.35, SEPA bank transfer €0.25, Bancontact 1.40% + €0.25 and Klarna 4.50% + €0.35. PayPal through Mollie costs PayPal's fees + €0.10. Mollie offers a no-subscription option (Pay as you go) and a Pro plan at €20/month.

### Alma

**Alma** specializes in installment and deferred payments. On its [pricing page](https://almapay.com/pricing): 3.60% excl. VAT for paying in 2 installments and 4.20% excl. VAT for deferred payment at D+15, with no setup or monthly fees on the standard offer (up to €1M in revenue). Part of the fees can be shared with the customer, within regulatory limits. Rates may be adjusted based on risk, with an automatic increase for American Express and business cards. Paying in 5 to 12 installments is on a waiting list. For the full comparison with Klarna, see our [Alma vs Klarna](/en/payments/alma-vs-klarna/) article.

### PayPal

According to [PayPal France's fees page](https://www.paypal.com/fr/business/paypal-business-fees): 2.90% + €0.35 per domestic commercial transaction. No surcharge for EEA buyers, 1.29% for UK buyers and 1.99% for other markets. Advanced card payments start at 1.20% plus fixed fees, American Express at 3.50%. A chargeback costs €16. Currency conversion is charged at 3.0% above the base exchange rate.

## How to choose

### You're on Shopify

Turn on **Shopify Payments** to avoid third-party transaction fees, then add **PayPal** if your customers ask for it and an installment solution like **Alma** if your average order value suits it.

### You have a custom-built store

**Stripe** is the cheaper of the two on a standard European card (1.5% vs 1.80% at Mollie). **Mollie** becomes attractive if a large share of your customers pay with Cartes Bancaires (1.20% + €0.25) or if you sell in Belgium and the Netherlands with Bancontact and iDEAL.

### You want installment payments

Compare the merchant cost: **Alma** from 3.60% excl. VAT in 2 installments, **Klarna** at 4.50% + €0.35 through Mollie or from 4.99% + €0.40 through Stripe. Then measure the effect on your own conversion before rolling it out everywhere.
