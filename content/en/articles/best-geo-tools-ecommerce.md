## How we ranked these tools

A GEO tool for a store has to answer three questions: do AI assistants mention my brand and products, why does a competitor come first, and what should I publish to fix it. Based on the vendors' official pages, we compared:

- **E-commerce features**: product catalog, Shopify, ChatGPT Shopping.
- **Taking action**: does the tool only measure, or does it create and publish content?
- **Engines tracked**, especially Google AI Overviews (where your shoppers are) and Mistral for the French market.
- **Price for 50 to 100 prompts**, a realistic volume for a store getting started.
- **Euro billing and free trial.**

We did not run a head-to-head test of data accuracy between tools. This ranking compares the offers as the vendors describe them.

## What is the best GEO tool for an e-commerce store?

### 1. Refine: measure, then publish what's missing

**Refine** tracks what AI assistants say about your brand and creates the content that gets you cited. The Visibility plan (€165/month) tracks 50 prompts daily on ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity. Autopilot (€321/month) moves to 200 prompts, adds Claude and Google AI Overviews, connects Search Console and GA4, and includes up to 30 optimized articles a month, with agents for content strategy and Reddit.

On the e-commerce side, the comparison table includes publishing to your Shopify blog from Autopilot. The Scale plan (custom pricing) adds AI-readable product pages (Product schema, FAQs, specs as text) and your products in ChatGPT Shopping. Both paid plans start with a 7-day free trial, no credit card required.

- **Right for you if** you want a tool that does the work after the diagnosis, on Shopify.
- **Less suited if** you only want a cheap tracking dashboard.

Pricing checked on [Refine's pricing page](https://getrefine.ai/pricing) on Oct 18, 2026.

### 2. Peec AI: product-by-product visibility

**Peec AI** is an analytics tool for visibility in AI answers. Starter costs €85/month (50 prompts), Pro €205/month (150 prompts) and Advanced €425/month (350 prompts). Standard plans track 3 engines of your choice among ChatGPT, AI Mode, AI Overviews, Copilot, Gemini and Naver AI. Enterprise goes up to 13 models, including Claude, Mistral and Perplexity.

Its strength for e-commerce is the AI Shopping module: a catalog imported from Shopify or CSV, visibility, position and win rate per product (SKU), and the domains AI retrieves when it recommends a product. The pricing page doesn't say which plans include this module. We found no content generation in the offer.

- **Right for you if** you have a catalog and want to know which products show up in AI answers.
- **Less suited if** you want a tool that writes and publishes content.

Pricing checked on [Peec AI's pricing page](https://peec.ai/pricing) on Oct 18, 2026.

### 3. Hikoo: the French option with many engines

**Hikoo** describes itself as a GEO platform for AI search, with data hosted in France. Monitoring costs €69/month (30 prompts, ChatGPT and Gemini), Growth €179/month (80 prompts, adds Perplexity and Copilot) and Agent €499/month (200 prompts). The Agent plan covers ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, Copilot, Mistral, Grok, AI Overviews and AI Mode. Annual billing gives 20% off, and the FAQ mentions a free plan.

Hikoo writes briefs, drafts missing pages and refreshes outdated ones (10, 30 or 150 suggestions a month depending on the plan), with automatic publishing or publishing after approval. CMS connections are "confirmed before activation": we found no list of CMSs and nothing Shopify-specific.

- **Right for you if** you sell mainly in France and want to track Mistral.
- **Less suited if** you need product-by-product tracking.

Pricing checked on [Hikoo's pricing page](https://www.tryhikoo.com/fr/tarifs/) on Oct 18, 2026. A third-party directory lists a top plan at €389: we use the price on the official page.

### 4. Mentionable: lots of prompts from the start

**Mentionable** offers Growth at €79/month, Pro at €149/month and Agency at €299/month, with up to 115, 231 or 385 prompts. All three plans track 7 engines: ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot, AI Mode and AI Overviews. That's the best prompt volume for the price among the tools compared.

The tool generates pages to fill visibility gaps (using credits), includes a Reddit engagement module on all plans and an e-commerce section (products, resellers). Shopify is not mentioned. 7-day trial, no card.

- **Right for you if** you want to track many questions on all major engines.
- **Less suited if** you want direct publishing to your store.

Pricing checked on [Mentionable's pricing page](https://mentionable.ai/pricing) on Oct 18, 2026.

### 5. Otterly.AI: the cheapest way in

**Otterly.AI**, run by an Austrian company, starts at $29/month (Lite, 15 prompts), then $189/month (Standard, 100 prompts) and $489/month (Premium, 400 prompts). Every plan covers ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity and Copilot. Google AI Mode, Gemini and Claude are paid add-ons.

Otterly offers content audits and recommendations, but no content generation or publishing. A Looker Studio connector, API and MCP are included from Standard. No e-commerce features are described.

- **Right for you if** you want a first tracking setup for a few dozen dollars.
- **Less suited if** you want to take action in the same tool.

Pricing checked on [Otterly.AI's pricing page](https://otterly.ai/pricing) on Oct 18, 2026.

### 6. Meteoria: per-product views

**Meteoria** offers Starter at €75/month (25 prompts tracked daily), Pro at €175/month (100), Advanced at €420/month (300) and Enterprise from €700/month. Each plan includes 3 engines, and the others (ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overview, Gemini, Grok, AI Mode, Copilot) cost extra.

The FAQ mentions per-product views, and "AI-friendly" content is included from Pro. Google Analytics is connected on all plans. The trial lasts one week.

Pricing checked on [Meteoria's pricing page](https://meteoria.ai/#tarifs) on Oct 18, 2026.

### 7. Qwairy: the widest choice of engines

**Qwairy**, a French company, works on credits. There's a free plan (120 credits), then Starter at €79/month, Growth at €199/month and Business at €449/month, with unlimited prompts and 2,500, 10,000 or 30,000 credits a month. Its engine list is the longest in this comparison: ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, Copilot, Grok, AI Overviews, AI Mode, Meta AI, Mistral, Qwen, Kimi and Alexa for Shopping.

Qwairy connects to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, and offers a Content Studio and an MCP from Starter.

Pricing checked on [Qwairy's pricing page](https://qwairy.co/pricing) on Oct 18, 2026.

### 8. Profound: for large enterprises

**Profound** no longer publishes prices: Enterprise is custom and tracks up to 9 engines, including ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Mode, Gemini, Copilot, Claude and AI Overviews. The free trial tracks 50 prompts for 7 days. For e-commerce, Profound tracks product visibility in ChatGPT Shopping at SKU level, an Enterprise-only feature.

Details checked on [Profound's pricing page](https://www.tryprofound.com/pricing) on Oct 18, 2026.

## Other tools worth knowing

- **Geoptie**: $41/month (15 prompts), $83/month (100) and $333/month (400). You pick 4 engines out of 7, content generation is unlimited and there are free tools ([pricing](https://geoptie.com/pricing)).
- **GetMint**: no public pricing. It advertises AI shopping visibility tracking, Amazon included ([pricing](https://getmint.ai/pricing)).
- **Semrush**: its AI Visibility Toolkit costs $99/month for 25 prompts, with no free trial ([Semrush docs](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1493-ai-toolkit)).

## How to choose your GEO tool

1. **You just want to know where you stand:** start with a free trial (Refine, Hikoo, Mentionable, Profound and Meteoria offer one) on 20 to 30 questions your customers really ask.
2. **You have a large catalog:** look first at product-level tracking (Peec AI, Profound, or Refine's product pages on Scale).
3. **You sell in France:** check Mistral tracking (Hikoo Agent, Qwairy, Peec AI Enterprise) and euro billing.
4. **You don't have time to create content:** pick a tool that writes and publishes (Refine, Hikoo, Mentionable) over a simple dashboard.
5. **Be wary of numbers based on a single answer:** AI answers vary from one run to the next. A good tool repeats each question and shows trends, not a fixed "#1 position".
