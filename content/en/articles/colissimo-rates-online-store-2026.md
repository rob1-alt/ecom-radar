## The 2026 Colissimo online grid

| Weight up to | Home delivery | Pickup point |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 250 g | €5.49 | €4.79 |
| 500 g | €7.59 | €6.89 |
| 750 g | €9.29 | €8.59 |
| 1 kg | €9.59 | €8.89 |
| 2 kg | €11.19 | €10.49 |
| 5 kg | €17.39 | €16.69 |
| 10 kg | €25.29 | Not offered |
| 15 kg | €31.99 | Not offered |
| 30 kg | €39.59 | Not offered |

Rates taken from the [Colissimo online rates page](https://www.laposte.fr/colissimo-en-ligne/tarifs) on November 2, 2026, from mainland France to mainland France (Corsica included), Monaco and Andorra. The page says "net rates" without specifying VAT: check at the time of shipping.

The [Colissimo page on La Poste Pro](https://www.laposte.fr/professionnel/tarif-colissimo) shows the same amounts for mainland France, "from January 1, 2026".

## What is included in these rates?

According to the rates page, postage is by weight, with proof of drop-off, and tracking is included. Home delivery is to the letterbox, without a signature, or with a counter-signature for parcels over 5 kg.

The page states a 2-working-day delivery commitment (no commitment to Andorra) and built-in compensation of €23/kg for loss or damage, postage included.

## How much does the pickup point save?

The gap is €0.70 on every band up to 5 kg. Here is the calculation for a 500 g parcel, based on the grid above:

| Monthly volume | Home (€7.59) | Pickup point (€6.89) | Difference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 100 parcels | €759 | €689 | €70 |
| 500 parcels | €3,795 | €3,445 | €350 |
| 1,000 parcels | €7,590 | €6,890 | €700 |

This assumes every parcel weighs 500 g and ships within mainland France. It does not tell you what your customers prefer: a pickup point means a trip, so offer it as an option rather than imposing it.

## What does the January 1, 2027 increase change?

In its [press release of July 27, 2026](https://www.caissedesdepots.fr/sites/ms-cdc-fr/files/2026-07/CP_La_Poste_tarifs_au_010127.pdf) (in French), La Poste announces that Colissimo rates will rise "on average by 4.1% in 2027 for shipments to mainland France and abroad". Parcels between mainland France and the overseas territories "will see a 5% rate decrease".

The release does not give the 2027 grid by weight band, and its Colissimo figures concern consumer shipments: nothing is said about business contracts. For illustration only, if that average applied as is to 500 home-delivered parcels of 500 g, the bill would go from about €3,795 to €3,951 a month (+€156). This is not a forecast: wait for the official grid.

## What if you have a Colissimo business contract?

The [Colissimo business site](https://www.colissimo.entreprise.laposte.fr) presents contracts, but without rates or a minimum volume. It highlights the Coliship tool for outbound and return labels, Retour Online for a customizable returns journey, and modules to display pickup points on your site. The page also claims more than 70,000 e-merchant customers and 20,000 pickup points.

A multi-carrier tool can also serve as a benchmark: see [Sendcloud vs Boxtal](/en/shipping/sendcloud-vs-boxtal/). The Colissimo rates shown by Sendcloud do not state the parcel weight, so do not compare them directly with the grid above.

## How to choose

- You ship a few dozen light parcels a month: the online grid is enough, with the pickup point as an option to save €0.70 per parcel.
- You ship several hundred parcels a month: ask Colissimo for a contract quote and compare it with a multi-carrier tool, presented in our [shipping software roundup](/en/shipping/best-ecommerce-shipping-software/).
- You want to cut shipping costs without changing rates: work on your return rate, explained in [how to reduce your online store's return rate](/en/returns-support/reduce-ecommerce-return-rate/).
