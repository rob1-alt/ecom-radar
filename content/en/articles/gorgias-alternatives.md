## Gorgias versus the alternatives

The key difference is not features but billing. According to the [Gorgias Shopify App Store listing](https://apps.shopify.com/helpdesk), **Gorgias** bills by ticket volume rather than per agent:

| Gorgias plan | Monthly price | Tickets included | Extra ticket |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | $10 | 50 | $0.40 per ticket (3 agents max) |
| Basic | $60 ($600/year) | 300 | $40 per 100 |
| Pro | $360 ($3,600/year) | 2,000 | $36 per 100 |
| Advanced | $900 ($9,000/year) | 5,000 | $36 per 100 |

Pricing taken from the [Gorgias Shopify App Store listing](https://apps.shopify.com/helpdesk) on Nov 5, 2026, in dollars. The AI agent is a separate module, paid per resolution according to the [Gorgias pricing page](https://www.gorgias.com/pricing). Details in our [Gorgias review: pricing, pros and cons, alternatives](/en/returns-support/gorgias-review-pricing/).

Looking for an alternative makes sense in two cases: ticket spikes blow up your bill, or you have few agents and want a per-seat price.

## The comparison

| Tool | Free plan | First paid plan | Billing | Shopify |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Freshdesk** | No (14-day trial) | Growth, $19/agent/month annual | Per agent | Free Shopify app |
| **Help Scout** | Yes, 5 users and 100 contacts/month | Standard, $25/user/month | Per user | Integration with order actions |
| **Tidio** | Yes, 50 billable conversations | Starter, $24.17/month | Per plan, based on conversations | Native Shopify actions from Growth |
| **Zendesk** | No | Suite Team, $55/agent/month annual ($69 monthly) | Per agent | App on the Shopify App Store |

Pricing checked on Nov 5, 2026 on the pricing pages of [Freshdesk](https://www.freshworks.com/freshdesk/pricing/), [Help Scout](https://www.helpscout.com/pricing/), [Tidio](https://www.tidio.com/pricing/) and [Zendesk](https://www.zendesk.com/pricing/). All in dollars. The Tidio price shown matches the annual option ("2 months free").

## What it costs at your volume

Calculated from the prices above. Gorgias is calculated on monthly billing on its Basic plan, the others on the per-agent price shown (annual for Freshdesk and Zendesk). Tidio is excluded: it bills by conversations, not by agents or tickets.

| Situation | **Gorgias** Basic | **Freshdesk** Growth | **Help Scout** Standard | **Zendesk** Suite Team |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3 agents, 300 tickets/month | $60 | $57 | $75 | $165 |
| 8 agents, 1,000 tickets/month | $340 ($60 + 700 extra tickets at $0.40) | $152 | $200 | $440 |

At 1,000 tickets a month, Gorgias Pro ($360, 2,000 tickets) costs slightly more than Basic with overage ($340). Freshdesk Pro ($55/agent) would come to $440 for 8 agents, the same as Zendesk.

## The alternatives in detail

### Freshdesk

According to its [pricing page](https://www.freshworks.com/freshdesk/pricing/), **Freshdesk** offers Growth ($19), Pro ($55) and Enterprise ($89) per agent per month on annual billing. Growth includes ticketing, a shared inbox and a knowledge base; Pro adds a multilingual helpdesk, intelligent routing and multiple SLA policies. The Freddy AI agent includes 500 sessions, then $49 per 100 extra sessions. The page does not mention Shopify: the [Freshworks Shopify app](https://freshworks.com/apps/shopify) is free and gives access to recent orders inside Freshdesk, though the page does not say which actions are possible.

### Help Scout

The [Help Scout pricing page](https://www.helpscout.com/pricing/) shows Free, Standard ($25), Plus ($45) and Pro ($75) per user per month, with unlimited contacts on paid plans. Its [Shopify integration](https://docs.helpscout.com/article/294-install) shows customer data in the sidebar (lifetime value, average order value, order details) and lets you refund, cancel, edit or duplicate recent orders. The documentation does not say which plans include it. The Envoy AI add-on costs $0.75 per resolution.

### Tidio

**Tidio** combines live chat, ticketing and AI. Its [pricing page](https://www.tidio.com/pricing/) lists a Free plan, Starter at $24.17/month, Growth from $49.17/month and Plus from $300/month plus usage. Native Shopify actions arrive with Growth. The Lyro AI is a separate module, from $32.50/month for 50 conversations. The limit to know: plans are counted in conversations, which suits high-volume email support less well.

### Zendesk

**Zendesk** pricing (Suite Team $55, Suite Professional $115 on annual billing) and the full calculation are in our [Gorgias vs Zendesk](/en/returns-support/gorgias-vs-zendesk/) head-to-head. AI resolutions beyond the quota cost $1.50 or $2 each.

## How to choose

- **Many agents, moderate ticket volume:** **Freshdesk** or **Help Scout** come out cheaper than Gorgias on the math.
- **Few tickets and a small budget:** the free plans of **Help Scout** and **Tidio**.
- **Support mostly through chat on your store:** **Tidio**.
- **You want to handle Shopify orders from the ticket, with volume-based billing:** stay with **Gorgias**.
